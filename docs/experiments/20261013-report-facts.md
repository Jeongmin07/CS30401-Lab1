## Main mechanism: T26.2 reordering and false loss inference

At 26.2 s the supplied forward path changes from **12 to 10 links/hops**, including the two GSLs. These are not counts of satellites. Rounded snapshots at 26.1/26.2 show propagation RTT 97.698/91.064 ms. The same-instant old/new route geometry in `forward-hints.csv` is 48.847704/45.531945 ms one way: an exact **3.315759 ms** decrease. The rounded snapshot difference and the same-instant calculation have different definitions.

At 10 Mbps, a serialized full data packet of 1,434 B takes **1.1472 ms**. The geometric decrease is about 2.89 such intervals, but that ratio does not prove a count of reordered or retransmitted packets. The actual tagged pair has TX-to-RX delays 88.298493/77.973720 ms, a TX gap of 4.588757 ms, and reverse arrival separation 5.736016 ms. Actual delay includes serialization, queues, and forwarding while the packet is in flight.

The baseline evidence records **three RETX events in one recovery episode**, not three recovery events. A SACK block covers three MSS above the missing head when recovery begins. The original tagged data arrives before the corresponding retransmission; the local window has no observed data or ACK drops. Describe the measured CUBIC/SACK/PRR course stack; do not claim the same loss behavior for all modern Linux TCP/RACK implementations.

The final selective mechanism retains K3 and briefly defers eligible old-flight presumed-loss holes under one shared deadline. In natural 200 s bulk, baseline/selective/selective+tail app goodput is **9.544356 / 9.5465088 / 9.5465088 Mbps**. In `[25.7,28.2)`, RETX/recovery changes **3/1 to 0/0**. The whole gain is only 0.02256%; the tail action count is zero in the unfinished 100 GB flow.

## Finite completion: repair a regression, not beat unmodified TCP

For 27,600 B (20 MSS), stable 100 Mbps, a real first loss, and deliberately incorrect positive route information:

The admission permits at most one MSS beyond the ordinary flight allowance, requires new unique SACK evidence, respects receiver allowance, leaves cwnd and the shared deadline unchanged at admission, and uses native sending/accounting. Later native cwnd trajectories may differ; do not say cwnd remains identical throughout the experiment.

## T75 queue mechanism and optional system control

The actual stopped/full source device is Seoul node 1584/device 1, GSL, 100-packet drop-tail, with no root qdisc or QueueLimits. Exact TC discard identities have device-stop set, BQL-stop unset, and 100 queued packets. The larger selective queue already exists before T75. Delaying only the T75 forwarding delta until T78 preserves pre-T75 history and still produces 11 drops, 12 RETX and two recoveries around T75. This establishes that this switch is not necessary for those local rejections; it does not uniquely identify all upstream causes or remove every LEO effect.

Keep `[74.5,77)` and `[74.5,77.5)` distinct: the unmodified baseline has zero drops in the former and 12 in the latter. Selective has 11 in both. Source served queue residence is not SRTT or end-to-end RTT.

Optional rate-capped native pacing preserves the tested long-bulk goodput (9.5465088 to 9.5662704 Mbps), reduces source post `[75,75.5)` wait/queue to zero across 436 served visits, and has zero observed tagged data losses. The network still has queueing, and source startup peaks at 10 queued packets. Short-flow FCT increases 83.27814%; the real startup-loss pair has equal 53.228969 ms first-loss retransmission delay but a 0.10163% goodput cost. Keep pacing OFF by default. This is an existing system-control mechanism with operator-known fixed capacity, not independent evidence for the orbit-aware algorithm.

## Reference and wording checks

Hypatia's primary paper already describes route-shortening reordering and false loss inference in Section 4.2; detecting that phenomenon is not a novelty claim. [Kassing et al., IMC 2020](https://bdebopam.github.io/papers/imc2020-hypatia.pdf), DOI [10.1145/3419394.3423635](https://doi.org/10.1145/3419394.3423635), supports the environment and problem motivation. [Handley's HotNets 2018 university record](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10062262/), DOI [10.1145/3286062.3286075](https://doi.org/10.1145/3286062.3286075), verifies publication metadata; a failed PDF fetch in this audit is not new full-text verification.

[RFC 9438](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9438.html) supports CUBIC's relevance as a deployed standard; it does not make this simulator's complete recovery stack identical to today's operating systems. [RFC 3042 Section 2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3042.html#section-2) is prior art for constrained new data on duplicate ACK/SACK feedback. [RFC 8985 Sections 3.2 and 7](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8985.html) is prior art for probing and time-based loss detection. The implemented one-final-original admission during forecast wait has different triggers and bounds; it does not implement either RFC wholesale.
